R ReachOut

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

ReachOut ("we", "the app") helps students and job seekers tailor their resume for different domains and send personalised emails to professors and recruiters from their own Gmail account. This policy explains what data we handle and why. ReachOut is an open-source project: github.com/paraskatiyar91-svg/reachout.

1. Data we collect

  • Google account basics: your name, email address and profile picture, used to sign you in.
  • Gmail send permission (gmail.send): lets the app send the emails you review and approve. ReachOut cannot read, search, modify or delete any email in your mailbox.
  • Profile and resume: the details, resume file and resume text you provide.
  • Contacts: the names, emails and notes in the spreadsheet you upload.
  • Your AI API keys (optional): Gemini, OpenAI or Claude keys you choose to connect.
  • Activity: drafts you create and a log of emails sent through the app.

2. How we use it

  • To generate tailored resumes and email drafts, and to send the emails you approve, from your Gmail, to the recipients you choose.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.
  • Emails are only sent after you approve them, at a limited rate.

3. Google user data (Limited Use)

ReachOut's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use the Gmail send permission solely to send messages you explicitly approve in the app. Google user data is not transferred to third parties except as needed to provide this feature, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans except with your consent, for security purposes, or where required by law.

4. AI providers

If you connect your own AI key, your resume text and the contact details needed for a draft are sent to your chosen provider (Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic) using your key, under that provider's terms. If you do not connect a key, no data is sent to any AI provider.

5. Storage and security

  • Gmail tokens, AI keys and uploaded resume files are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM).
  • All traffic uses HTTPS. Sessions are protected against cross-site attacks.
  • The app is hosted on Render. During the free-hosting phase, stored data may be reset when the server restarts.

6. Your controls

  • Disconnect Gmail at any time from your Profile. This revokes the app's access at Google. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Remove AI keys at any time from your Profile.
  • Delete your account from your Profile. This permanently deletes your profile, resume, contacts, drafts, keys and logs.

7. Your responsibilities

Only upload contacts you have a legitimate reason to email, and respect any request not to be contacted again. See the Terms of Service.

8. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/paraskatiyar91-svg/reachout/issues.
